Dear WOCC Admissions Committee,

I am writing to apply for the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Contemporary Challenges in the
World of Work (WOCC). As a Total Rewards and Compensation specialist who has spent the past
six years building, digitising and governing the reward architecture of one of Bangladesh's
largest conglomerates (25+ business units, ~5,000 payroll employees), I have seen first-hand
how work is being transformed — by automation, by data, by policy, and by the shifting
relationship between employers and employees. WOCC's interdisciplinary lens on labour,
employment and the changing world of work is exactly the analytical foundation I need to move
from expert practitioner to a strategic, internationally-minded HR leader.

**Why this programme, why now.** My career has grown within a single large employer, from
payroll officer to leading the Reward Management & Compensation & Benefits Centre of
Excellence. In that role I own salary benchmarking, pay-structure and grade design, incentive
and bonus governance, PF/gratuity, variable pay, and compensation analytics — and I report to
the CPO and Group CEO on people-cost decisions. The further I rise, the more I am asked to
reason about labour markets, public policy, and the fairness and future of work — not just
about spreadsheets and pay bands. WOCC's four pillars (global dynamics of work and employment,
public policy, contemporary issues such as digital transformation and mental health at work,
and critical-analytical skills) speak directly to that gap. I want to study these questions in
an international consortium, compare how work is organised across different labour-market
models, and return equipped to lead reward and people strategy in a way that is both
data-rigorous and human.

**What I would contribute.** I am not coming as a recent graduate but as a working HR
professional who has implemented the very systems this programme analyses. Among my concrete
achievements:
- Building and running the Reward CoE across a 25+ SBU conglomerate, with end-to-end monthly
  payroll governance for ~5,000 employees.
- Leading salary benchmarking and pay-structure/job-evaluation work, incentive and bonus
  governance, and PF/gratuity administration under Bangladesh Labour Law and income-tax
  compliance.
- Automating HR/payroll workflows on ERP (iBOS) and PeopleDesk — including building an
  internal-equity and pay-compression analysis engine and running performance-and-potential
  assessment into a variable-pay matrix.
- Partnering as HRBP to seven trading businesses, translating business plans into manpower
  forecasts and workforce cost control.
- Executing HR transformation, SOP/process-governance and special projects directly assigned
  by the CPO and Group CEO.

Across six business units I have designed assessment centres, driven employee engagement and
employer-branding initiatives, and supported appraisal calibration — the "people" side of work
that balances my "pay" expertise.

**My goals after WOCC.** I intend to build a career at the intersection of compensation
strategy, workforce analytics and labour-policy awareness — ultimately as a regional HR/Total
Rewards leader or CHRO-track executive who can advise on pay, workforce planning and the
future of work across borders. WOCC's mobility across France, and partner universities in
Belgium, Spain, Italy, Canada and Argentina, would give me the comparative, multilingual
perspective that role demands.
